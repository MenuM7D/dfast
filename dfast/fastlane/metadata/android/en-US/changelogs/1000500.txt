Every page shows its content again.

After a bottom-dock tab was tapped, the page area collapsed to zero height and
the app looked like an empty screen with only the dock. The page container was
anchored to a view that the new dock wrapper had moved out of its layout, so the
constraint no longer resolved.

The dock now anchors the pages correctly, the whole dock card hides and returns
as one piece, and the keyboard no longer leaves an empty card behind.
