DFast 1.1.0 — Flux

- A complete new interface: floating dock navigation, a hero search on Home,
  cinematic media cards, a tile-based hub and a rebuilt welcome flow.
- New DFast mark and launcher icon in indigo and aqua.
- Fixed clipped Arabic labels: the type scale no longer applies letter spacing,
  and the dock never squeezes a label into a single ellipsized line.
- Everything from before is still here: search, downloads, queue, terminal,
  cookies, command templates, sources, logs and settings.
